Questions

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and Home Assistant running at home. Deck OS works with whatever devices your Home Assistant already has.

Does my data leave my house?

No. Deck OS runs on your computer and talks to your own Home Assistant. The only thing it sends out is a weekly check of your license key.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, through Tailscale (free), which links your phone to your home securely. Deck OS turns away everyone else on the internet.

How do parental controls work?

Invite someone as a Kid and you choose which features and devices they can use, plus a bedtime. Kids can see locks and the garage but can never unlock or open them.

What can I do in the trial?

Look around Deck OS with a made-up home, talk to Elara (she runs that home for you), and try the basics (themes, lists, notes, calendar). Connecting your own Home Assistant and the full features need the license key that comes with your purchase ($39 once). After 5 days, Deck OS asks for that key; nothing you set up is deleted.

Can I add my own features?

Yes. Deck OS was built with Claude Code, and your copy is yours to change. Open Claude Code in your Deck OS folder and describe what you want ("add a page that tracks my dog's vet visits") and it can build it in.

Do you help with setup or customizing?

Deck OS is sold as-is. The setup wizard walks you through everything, and for anything beyond that, Claude Code (or any AI coding assistant) can help you change your copy however you like.

Can guests use it?

Yes. Give someone staying with you a guest pass and they can talk to Elara and use what you allow (like the lights in their room or the music) while they're there. They can never unlock doors or see your money or private info, and the pass ends when they leave.

Is it a security system?

No. Deck OS is a convenience app. Keep your real alarms, smoke detectors and locks working on their own.
